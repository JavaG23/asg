## About A Simple Gesture

A Simple Gesture is a nonprofit food rescue program that collects non-perishable food donations from residential donors and delivers them to local food pantries. Our volunteer drivers make pickups easy by coming directly to your door.

---

## How It Works

### 1. Sign Up
You receive a donation bag from ASG (delivered by a volunteer driver).

### 2. Fill Your Bag
Throughout the month, add non-perishable food items to your bag.

### 3. Set Out Your Donation
On pickup day, place your filled bag outside your front door by the designated time.

### 4. We Pick It Up
A volunteer driver collects your donation and delivers it to a local food pantry.

### 5. Repeat
Your empty bag will be returned for next month's donation.

## Preparing Your Donation

### Where to Place Your Bag
- Front porch or doorstep (most common)
- Near your front door where it's visible
- Protected from weather if possible
- Accessible to volunteers (not behind locked gates)

### When to Set It Out
- Place your bag outside by **8:00 AM** on pickup day
- Check your email for specific pickup date reminders
- Pickups typically occur between 9:00 AM and 12:00 PM

### Special Instructions
If your home has special access requirements:
- Gate codes
- Hidden driveways
- Preferred pickup location

Contact your ASG coordinator to update your profile.

## Pickup Days

### Scheduled Dates
ASG pickups occur on scheduled event days throughout the year. Check your email for upcoming dates or contact your local coordinator.

### Missing a Pickup
If you can't participate in a pickup:
- No action needed - just don't set out your bag
- Your spot will be included in the next pickup
- You don't need to notify anyone

### Pickup Confirmation
After your donation is collected, you may receive a confirmation email with:
- Confirmation that your donation was picked up
- Thank you message
- Next scheduled pickup date

## Donation Guidelines

### Accepted Items
We accept **non-perishable food items** including:

**Canned Goods:**
- Vegetables (corn, beans, peas, tomatoes)
- Fruits
- Soups and stews
- Tuna, chicken, salmon
- Beans (black, kidney, pinto)

**Dry Goods:**
- Rice and pasta
- Cereal and oatmeal
- Peanut butter
- Crackers
- Granola bars

**Other Items:**
- Cooking oil
- Condiments (unopened)
- Juice boxes
- Shelf-stable milk

### Not Accepted
Please **do not** donate:
- Expired items
- Opened packages
- Perishable foods (fresh produce, dairy, meat)
- Home-canned items
- Items without labels
- Non-food items (unless specifically requested)

### Packaging Tips
- Keep items in the ASG bag provided
- Don't overfill the bag (volunteers need to carry it)
- Glass items should be cushioned to prevent breakage
- Multiple bags are okay if you have extra donations

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What if I don't have the ASG bag?
Use any bag or box for your donation. Contact your coordinator to request a replacement ASG bag.

### What if the volunteer didn't pick up my donation?
If your donation wasn't collected:
1. Check that it was placed in a visible location
2. Verify the pickup date was correct
3. Contact your ASG coordinator

### Can I donate money instead of food?
Yes! Financial donations are always welcome and help cover program costs. Contact A Simple Gesture directly for donation options.

### How do I change my contact information?
Contact your ASG coordinator with updated information, including:
- New address
- New phone number
- New email address

### Can I skip a month?
Yes, participation is voluntary. Simply don't set out your bag on pickup day.

### What happens to my donation?
Your donated food goes directly to local food pantries in your community, helping families in need.

### How do I stop participating?
Contact your ASG coordinator if you wish to be removed from the pickup list.
