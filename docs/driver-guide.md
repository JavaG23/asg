## Getting Started

### Installing the App

For the best experience, install the app on your phone:

**On iPhone:**
1. Open Safari and go to the app URL
2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow)
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Tap **Add**

**On Android:**
1. Open Chrome and go to the app URL
2. Tap the menu (three dots)
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Tap **Add**

### Logging In

1. Open the app
2. Tap **Sign In**
3. Enter your email address
4. Enter your password
5. Tap **Sign In**

If you forgot your password:
1. Tap **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email
3. Check your email for reset link
4. Set a new password

## Your Dashboard

When you log in, you'll see your dashboard with:

### Route Information
- **Route Name**: Your assigned route for today
- **Progress Bar**: Shows how many stops you've completed
- **Stop Count**: "X of Y stops completed"

### Next Stop Card
Shows your next pickup address with:
- Street address
- City, State, ZIP
- Any special instructions

### Actions
- **Start Route / Continue**: Begin navigation to next stop
- **View All Stops**: See the complete list of addresses

## Completing Pickups

### Step 1: Navigate to the Address

1. From your dashboard, tap **Start Route** (or **Continue**)
2. You'll see the address details
3. Tap **Navigate to This Address**
4. Google Maps opens with directions

### Step 2: At the Pickup Location

When you arrive:
1. Look for the food donation (usually on porch or by door)
2. If there's food, collect it
3. Note any special circumstances

### Step 3: Confirm the Pickup

After collecting the food:
1. Return to the ASG app
2. Answer **"Was the food outside?"**
   - Tap **Yes** if food was there
   - Tap **No** if no food was found
3. Add any notes (optional)
4. Tap **Complete & Go to Next**

**If No Food Found:**
- When you tap "No", you'll be reminded to leave a note on the door
- This lets the donor know you stopped by

### Step 4: Continue to Next Stop

The app automatically loads your next stop. Repeat the process until all stops are complete.

## Bag Delivery Routes

If you're assigned a **Bag Delivery** route, your task is to deliver donation bags to new donors.

### Differences from Pickup Routes:

| Aspect | Pickup Route | Bag Delivery Route |
|--------|--------------|-------------------|
| Your task | Collect food | Deliver bags |
| Confirmation | "Was food outside?" | "Confirm delivery" |
| After completion | Go to weigh-in | You're done! |

### Completing a Bag Delivery:

1. Navigate to the address
2. Leave the donation bag (at door, porch, etc.)
3. Tap **Confirm Delivery & Go to Next**
4. Add any notes if needed
5. Continue to next address

## After Your Route

### When All Stops Are Done

After completing your last stop, you'll see a celebration screen. Your route status changes to **Pending Weight**.

### Go to Distribution Center

1. Tap **Navigate to Distribution Center**
2. Drive to the food pantry/distribution center
3. The coordinator will weigh your collected food
4. Once weighed, your route is marked complete

### What Happens Next

- Your volunteer hours are logged automatically
- The route is archived for records
- You can view your profile and past routes

## Tips for Success

### Before You Start

- Make sure your phone is charged
- Have the ASG app open and logged in
- Review your route stops
- Know where the distribution center is

### During Your Route

- Keep the app open while driving (use phone mount)
- Return to the app after each navigation
- Note any issues in the comments field
- Don't rush - safety first!

### Time Management

- Routes typically take 1-2 hours
- Allow time to get to distribution center
- Contact admin if running very late

### What to Collect

- Food items in bags or boxes on porches
- Look for ASG bags from previous deliveries
- Don't take items that look like regular packages/deliveries
- When in doubt, leave a note

### Handling Issues

| Situation | What to Do |
|-----------|-----------|
| No food at address | Mark "No", leave note on door |
| Can't find address | Check GPS, call admin if needed |
| Gate/access code needed | Check special instructions, call admin |
| Bad weather | Use caution, safety first |
| Car trouble | Contact admin immediately |

## Troubleshooting

### App Not Loading

1. Check your internet connection
2. Close and reopen the app
3. Try refreshing the page
4. Log out and log back in

### Can't See My Route

- Make sure you're logged in with correct email
- Check with admin that you're assigned to a route
- Route may be for a different date

### Navigation Not Working

- Allow location permissions when prompted
- Make sure Google Maps is installed
- Try tapping Navigate button again

### Lost My Password

1. On login screen, tap **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email address
3. Check email (including spam folder)
4. Click the reset link
5. Set a new password

### Stop Already Completed

- If you accidentally completed a stop, contact admin
- They can reset the stop status if needed

### App Crashes

1. Close the app completely
2. Reopen from home screen
3. If problem persists, try uninstalling and reinstalling
