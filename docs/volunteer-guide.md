## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account
1. Visit the ASG App URL provided by your coordinator
2. Click **Sign In**
3. Enter your email and password
4. Complete your profile information

### Installing the App
For easy access, install the app on your phone:

**iPhone:**
1. Open Safari and visit the app URL
2. Tap the Share button
3. Select **Add to Home Screen**
4. Tap **Add**

**Android:**
1. Open Chrome and visit the app URL
2. Tap the menu (three dots)
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Tap **Add**

## Volunteer Roles

### Driver
Drivers collect food donations from residential donors and bring them to the distribution center.

**Responsibilities:**
- Navigate to assigned pickup locations
- Collect donation bags from porches/doorsteps
- Mark pickups as complete in the app
- Deliver collected food to distribution center
- Participate in weigh-in process

**Requirements:**
- Valid driver's license
- Reliable vehicle
- Smartphone with the ASG app
- Available for 2-3 hours on event day

**[See Driver Guide for detailed instructions](/driver/help)**

### Distribution Center Volunteer
Help process donations at the distribution center.

**Responsibilities:**
- Receive food deliveries from drivers
- Weigh and record food amounts
- Sort donations by category
- Pack boxes for food pantries
- Maintain organized workspace

**Requirements:**
- Ability to lift moderate weight
- Available for scheduled shift
- Comfortable working on feet

### Administrative Volunteer
Help with behind-the-scenes coordination.

**Responsibilities:**
- Data entry and record keeping
- Donor communications
- Route planning assistance
- Volunteer coordination
- Event setup and planning

**Requirements:**
- Computer skills
- Attention to detail
- Good communication skills

## Event Day Overview

### Before the Event
1. Check your email for event date and assignment
2. Confirm your participation
3. Review your assigned role/route
4. Charge your phone and download the app

### During the Event

**For Drivers:**
1. Arrive at staging area (if applicable)
2. Start your route via the app
3. Navigate to each pickup location
4. Collect donations and mark complete
5. Deliver to distribution center
6. Participate in weigh-in

**For Center Volunteers:**
1. Arrive at your scheduled time
2. Check in with the coordinator
3. Complete assigned tasks
4. Log your hours

### After the Event
1. Ensure all tasks are complete
2. Log your volunteer hours
3. Provide feedback (if requested)
4. Check for next event date

## Logging Your Hours

### Automatic Tracking (Drivers)
If you're a driver using the app, your hours are tracked automatically:
- Start time: When you begin your route
- End time: When your route is weighed in
- Hours are logged to our volunteer tracking system

### Manual Logging (Other Roles)
For non-driver volunteer roles:
1. Log in to the app
2. Go to **My Hours** (coming soon)
3. Enter your shift details:
   - Date
   - Start time
   - End time
   - Role/activity
4. Submit for approval

### Hour Reports
You can view your volunteer history:
- Total hours volunteered
- Events participated
- Contributions made

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I sign up for an event?
Contact your local ASG coordinator or check your email for event announcements with signup links.

### What if I can't make my scheduled shift?
Notify your coordinator as soon as possible so they can find a replacement or adjust the schedule.

### Can I volunteer with a friend/family member?
Yes! Many volunteers participate together. Let your coordinator know when signing up.

### Do I need any special training?
- **Drivers**: Quick app orientation (self-guided via Help section)
- **Center volunteers**: Brief on-site training provided
- **Admin volunteers**: Training provided as needed

### What should I wear?
Comfortable clothes appropriate for:
- Walking (for drivers)
- Lifting/moving (for center volunteers)
- Weather conditions (outdoor loading areas)

Closed-toe shoes recommended for safety.

### Is there an age requirement?
- Drivers must be 18+ with valid license
- Center volunteers: 14+ (under 18 with adult)
- Administrative: Varies by task

### How do I update my availability?
Contact your coordinator with changes to your:
- Schedule availability
- Contact information
- Role preferences

### What if I have questions during an event?
- Check the Help section in the app
- Contact your coordinator
- Ask the on-site event lead
